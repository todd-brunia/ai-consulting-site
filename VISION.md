# Vision

> The maintained product reference is [`docs/product-brief.md`](docs/product-brief.md).
> This document is retained as the original vision and historical context.

## Purpose

Create a simple, credible website for a small AI workflow consulting practice.

The site should help small and medium businesses move from assistive AI usage
to practical AI-enabled workflows through low-risk experimentation,
education, and fixed-scope engagements with defined outcomes.

## Audience

- Small and medium businesses
- Technology managers
- Business leaders
- Operators who know AI may help but are unsure where to start

## Positioning

Many SMBs are already using assistive AI tools, but have not taken the leap
into AI-enabled workflows, automation, or agentic systems because the space is
confusing and the risks are unclear.

This practice helps those businesses take the next step safely and
confidently. The consultant should be positioned as an experienced software
engineer with practical AI understanding, sound technical judgment, and the
ability to turn confusing new AI capabilities into small, concrete experiments.

Do not position the consultant as an all-knowing AI expert. The tone should
acknowledge that the field is still evolving while making clear that strong
software engineering fundamentals are valuable for evaluating and applying
these tools responsibly.

## Engagement Model

Start small. Package work around defined outcomes instead of hourly billing.

Engagements should align with a step-by-step SMB journey into agentic AI:

- Education and orientation
- Workflow discovery
- Simple proofs of concept
- Low-risk automation experiments
- Practical demos that show what is possible
- Follow-on implementation only when there is a clear business case

## Goals

- Explain agentic AI in plain business language
- Demonstrate practical workflows
- Generate qualified leads
- Showcase engineering expertise
- Build trust through clarity, restraint, and useful examples

## Voice

- Practical
- Clear
- Grounded
- Low-hype
- Specific

Prefer concrete workflow examples over abstract transformation language.
Explain business value before technical implementation details.
