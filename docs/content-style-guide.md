# Content Style Guide

## Voice

Write as a practical, experienced engineer speaking to a thoughtful business or
technology leader. Sound clear, grounded, candid, and helpful. Use first person
when Todd is speaking and address the visitor directly when it improves clarity.

## Writing Rules

- Lead with the visitor's work, problem, or outcome.
- Use plain business language before technical terminology.
- Prefer concrete workflows and deliverables to abstract transformation claims.
- Keep headings informative and paragraphs easy to scan.
- Use “AI-enabled workflow” or a specific capability when “agentic AI” would be
  unclear.
- Describe uncertainty, constraints, risk, and human oversight honestly.
- Keep calls to action specific, low pressure, and consistent with a discovery
  conversation.

## Claims

Every claim must be supported by the product brief, current site, or explicit
input from Todd. Never invent clients, results, testimonials, credentials,
partnerships, or experience. Avoid “revolutionary,” “cutting-edge,” “guaranteed,”
“effortless,” and similarly inflated language.

## Review

Read new copy aloud, verify that it sounds like Todd, and check that a visitor
can identify who the practice helps, the problem addressed, the engagement
offered, and the next action.
