# Design Principles

- Professional rather than futuristic
- Practical rather than visionary
- Midwest credibility rather than Silicon Valley hype
- Clear language rather than AI jargon
- Strong typography and whitespace
- Limited animation
- Mobile-first layouts that remain usable at narrow widths
- Accessible color contrast, focus states, landmarks, and control labels
- Existing visual patterns before new one-off styles
- Every page and major section should lead toward a clear next action

Use restrained colors, simple geometry, and consistent spacing. Avoid generic
AI imagery, glowing networks, robots, excessive gradients, or motion without a
visitor benefit. Visual changes must be reviewed on desktop and mobile through
the Vercel preview before merge.
