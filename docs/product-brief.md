# Product Brief

## Purpose and Audience

Todd Brunia's consulting practice helps small and mid-sized organizations,
especially Midwestern business leaders, technology managers, software teams,
and operators, evaluate and adopt practical AI-enabled workflows.

Visitors generally believe AI may be useful but are unsure which processes are
good candidates, how to manage risk, or how to move beyond individual assistant
tools without committing to an oversized transformation program.

## Problems and Services

The practice helps visitors identify bounded workflow opportunities, evaluate
whether AI is appropriate, test important assumptions, and plan a maintainable
next step. Current engagement options are:

- AI Opportunity Assessment
- Agent Prototype Sprint
- AI Architecture & Readiness Review

Work should be fixed-scope and outcome-oriented. It may stop after discovery,
assessment, or a prototype when further investment is not justified.

## Desired Visitor Action

The primary action is to email Todd about one document-heavy,
coordination-heavy, or knowledge-intensive workflow. The conversation should
feel useful and low pressure, not like a sales presentation.

## Differentiators

- Practical engineering judgment rather than AI evangelism
- More than 20 years of enterprise software experience
- Staff Software Engineer experience
- AWS, cloud, technical leadership, and team mentoring experience
- Small experiments, human oversight, and explicit risk evaluation
- Willingness to recommend conventional automation or no new technology

## Voice and Personality

Use clear, specific, grounded language. The site should feel professional,
thoughtful, candid, and approachable. Explain the business value before the
implementation. Prefer evidence and reasonable uncertainty to sweeping claims.

## Claim Boundaries

Do not invent or imply clients, case studies, financial results, certifications,
awards, credentials, partnerships, or consulting outcomes. Do not mention
Todd's current employer or any client, prospect, or business area that could
create a conflict of interest. Do not imply that AI is always the right answer
or that outcomes are guaranteed.
